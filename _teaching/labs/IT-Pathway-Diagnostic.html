Which part of ITis actually yours?

Information technology isn't one job — it's ten different kinds of work that happen to share a building. This diagnostic measures two things separately: what you're drawn to, and how you already reason. Then it names the certifications that turn your top paths into a credential.

Part 1 — Interest · 40 items

Statements about the kind of work you'd choose. There is no right answer. Rate how much each one sounds like you.

Part 2 — Reasoning · 80 items

Short problems, 8 per area, one correct answer each. You are not expected to know any IT yet — every item can be reasoned out from scratch.

The full form measures each area on a nine-point scale; the short form on five. Use the short form when class time is tight.

About 25–35 minutes. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere — your answers stay in this browser tab, so finish in one sitting and print or save the results at the end.
