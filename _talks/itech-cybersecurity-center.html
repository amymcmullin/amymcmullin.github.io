About the Center

What the Center Does

The Center is a virtual home for cybersecurity at iTECH. It is led from the Computer Systems & Information Technology department and shares an external advisory board with iTECH's IT programs.

🧭

Program Guidance & Oversight

Keeps the cybersecurity pathway aligned to Florida DOE curriculum frameworks, current certification objectives and what local employers tell our advisory board they need.

🛡️

Cyber Defense Information

Curated, current guidance from CISA, NIST and other public sources for students, staff and the community.

🤝

Collaboration & Outreach

Connects students, faculty, other iTECH programs, our sister campus Lorenzo Walker Technical College, and regional industry partners.

🎓

Certification on Campus

iTECH's authorized Pearson VUE and Certiport testing center lets students earn industry certifications without leaving campus.

Programs of Study

The Cybersecurity Pathway

Students build their foundation in Computer Systems & IT before moving into Applied Cybersecurity. Local employers asked for this sequence: you can't defend a network you don't understand.

Step 1 · Foundation · FL DOE Y100200

Computer Systems & Information Technology

Hardware, operating systems, networking, Linux, troubleshooting and help-desk practice on real equipment, including in-house network trainers and the AI Innovation Lab's stations.
