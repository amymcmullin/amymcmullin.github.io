A web-based assessment that measures student interest and aptitude across ten IT areas: support and hardware, programming, networking, security, database design, data analytics, cloud, virtualization, AI, and project management.

Results match each path to industry certifications from Florida's CAPE funding lists, sorted by difficulty and color-coded by adult and dual-enrollment eligibility.
