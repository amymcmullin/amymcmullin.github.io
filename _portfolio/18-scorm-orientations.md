Three SCORM 1.2 course packages built for Canvas LMS, one each for iTECH's Medical Administrative Assistant, Accounting Technology, and Digital Media & Multimedia Design programs. Each package uses a six-SCO structure with tab-based navigation, knowledge checks, and SCORM reporting.

These were produced under deadline as evidence artifacts for our Council on Occupational Education accreditation. I specified SCORM 1.2 rather than a Canvas common cartridge because it carries completion and score reporting back to the LMS, which is what the accreditation evidence needed. Turnaround was same-day on administrator requests, and the packages were shared with our sister campus.

*The packages were generated with AI assistance against my specifications; I adapted several by hand for other programs. The format decision, the structure, the adaptation and the implementation are mine.*
