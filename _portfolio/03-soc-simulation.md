A roughly three-hour security operations center simulation built on Huntress Labs sandbox data. New students work as Tier 1 analysts and returning students as Tier 2, moving together through triage, investigation, and a hands-on response and containment block.

Materials include a facilitator guide, a student packet, and printable card sets.
